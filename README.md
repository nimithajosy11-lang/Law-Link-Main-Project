# ⚖️ Law Link – Online Legal Consultation Platform

## 📌 About the Project

**Law Link** is an online legal consultation and case management platform designed to connect users with professional advocates for legal assistance. It provides a convenient and organized digital solution for legal consultations, case requests, document management, and communication between clients and advocates.

The platform has three main user roles: **Admin, Advocate, and User**. Each role has specific responsibilities to ensure efficient management of legal services and case-related activities.

Law Link aims to simplify the process of finding suitable legal professionals, submitting case details, tracking case progress, and communicating with advocates through a single platform.

## 🎯 Objectives

- ⚖️ Connect users with professional advocates.
- 🔍 Help users find advocates based on legal case categories.
- 📝 Allow users to submit legal consultation requests.
- 📂 Support document uploads and case document management.
- 💬 Improve communication between clients and advocates.
- 📊 Track case status and progress.
- 🔐 Support secure management of user information and payment details.
- ⭐ Collect ratings and feedback to improve service quality.

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 User Module

- User registration and login.
- Search for advocates based on legal categories.
- Submit case details and descriptions.
- Upload relevant legal documents.
- Track case status and progress.
- Communicate with advocates through chat.
- Manage payment details.
- Rate advocates and provide feedback.

### 👨‍⚖️ Advocate Module

- Advocate registration and profile management.
- View submitted case requests.
- Accept or reject case requests.
- Communicate with clients.
- Update case progress and status.
- Manage case-related documents.
- Handle payment-related information.

### 🛡️ Admin Module

- Manage user and advocate registrations.
- Approve user and advocate accounts.
- Manage IPC sections and legal case categories.
- Monitor platform activities.
- Manage feedback and ratings.
- Oversee the overall system.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. Users register and log in to the platform.
2. Users search for advocates based on legal case categories.
3. Users submit case details, descriptions, and supporting documents.
4. Advocates review requests and accept or reject them.
5. Once a request is accepted, clients and advocates can communicate.
6. Advocates update case progress and manage relevant documents.
7. Users can track case status and provide ratings and feedback.
8. Administrators manage accounts, legal categories, and platform activities.
